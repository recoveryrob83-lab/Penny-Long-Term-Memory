## Standing Coordination Rules

- `Business_HQ` owns parent strategy, market selection, positioning, portfolio choices, offer strategy, customer-value judgment, commercial priorities, and Business-owned durable records.
- `Office_Leaks_HQ` owns Office Leaks files and execution continuity even while the business unit is paused.
- `Chief_of_Staff_HQ` receives reports, routes assignments, coordinates, and follows through without owning Business strategy or records.
- `Maintenance_HQ` owns global GitHub maintenance, shared governance, Boot integrity, audits, source-boundary enforcement, and reconciliation.
- `Engineering_HQ` owns technical architecture and implementation when concrete.
- `Finance_HQ` owns concrete pricing feasibility, financial risk, costs, taxes, benefits interactions, and affordability.
- `LifeOS_HQ` is the shared meeting room and has no independent backlog.
- Business-sourced advisories use `coordination/boards/business.md`, `coordination/ADVISORY_INDEX.md`, and the centralized V2 courier-envelope contract when transport applies.
- Do not update Department Event Inbox, Todoist, GitHub Issues, target boards, or department open loops merely for advisory visibility.
- Lifecycle state and priority remain separate.
- No dedicated standing Business_HQ Worker is authorized.
